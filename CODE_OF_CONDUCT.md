# Code of Conduct (lite)

RCOS is a commons for compounding-agent research. Keep it usable:

1. **Be decent.** No harassment, hate speech, doxxing, spam, or sexual content.
2. **Critique work, not people.** Technical disagreement is welcome; personal
   attacks are not.
3. **Assume good faith** until there is clear evidence otherwise.
4. **No security harm.** Do not post credentials, private data, or exploit
   payloads — see CONTRIBUTING.md security hygiene.
5. **Stay on topic.** Issues/PRs are about RCOS; take unrelated discussion elsewhere.

**Enforcement:** maintainers may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently
remove anyone who persistently degrades the commons. Reports: open a private
issue or contact a maintainer directly. Appeals are heard by a different
maintainer than the one who acted.
