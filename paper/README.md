# paper/ — two-stage publication plan (AGREED)

No hype, no AGI claims. Numbers only with benchmark logs behind them.

## Stage 1 — architecture / position preprint

Locks definitions, system boundaries, and the eval protocol before any results
exist. Content: what RCOS is (workflow-first control plane + heterogeneous
executable capabilities + eval-driven promotion/retirement), what it is not
(not a new self-improving-workflow invention), the three benchmark families,
and the pre-registered compounding experiment. Files: `outline.md` (12-section
skeleton), `abstract.md` (conservative abstract).

## Stage 2 — empirical systems paper

Written only after the prototype + Fam-C trajectory exist. Adds the
longitudinal overtaking result (RCOS starts equal/worse, beats fixed harnesses
as experience accumulates), the Fam-N transfer analysis, ablations (registry
off, retirement off, reuse-logging off), and cost accounting.
